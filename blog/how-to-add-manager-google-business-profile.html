How to Add Me as a Manager to Your Google Business Profile

By Sudeep Bagaria | Published on September 26, 2025

Running a medical practice in Nashik is demanding enough without having to wrestle with online listings. If you’ve decided to let me manage your Google Business Profile (GBP)—thank you! Granting me manager access is the safest way to let me optimize your profile while you keep full ownership and control.

Adding a manager takes only a few minutes and doesn’t require sharing your personal Google password. Here’s everything you need to know.

Why Manager Access Matters

A manager role allows me to update clinic hours, services, and contact details quickly; add or refresh photos of your practice; respond to patient reviews promptly; and post updates that keep your profile active. You remain the Primary Owner at all times and can remove managers whenever you wish.

Step-by-Step: Adding a Manager

  1. Sign in to Google Business Profile: Go to business.google.com and log in with the Google account you used to create your clinic’s profile.
  2. Select Your Clinic: If you have more than one location, choose the correct listing.
  3. Open Business Settings: Click Settings (gear icon) → Managers or Business Profile settings → People & access (Google sometimes changes the label).
  4. Invite a Manager: Click Add or Invite new user, enter my email sudeepbagaria1@gmail.com, and select Manager as the role.
  5. Send the Invitation: Click Invite. Google will email me and, once accepted, I can begin optimizing your profile.

Keeping Your Control

You can remove or change my role anytime under People & access. Only the Primary Owner can delete the profile or transfer ownership, and I will never ask for your Google password—this method keeps your account secure.

Tips for a Smooth Process

  • Use a desktop or laptop for easier navigation.
  • Double-check that you’re signed in to the correct Google account.
  • If you don’t see “Managers,” look for “Users”—the steps are the same.

The Bottom Line

Giving me manager access lets you stay focused on patient care while I handle the day-to-day updates of your Google Business Profile. Accurate information and prompt review responses help patients find you and trust your practice.
